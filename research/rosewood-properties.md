## 1. Rosewood Hong Kong

### Address + neighborhood + signature
- **Address:** Victoria Dockside, 18 Salisbury Road, Tsim Sha Tsui, Kowloon, Hong Kong
- **Neighborhood:** Tsim Sha Tsui, on the harbourfront of Kowloon. The property sits inside the US$2.6B Victoria Dockside development, next to the K11 MUSEA art mall and directly on the extended Avenue of Stars promenade [secondary: Wikipedia — Victoria Dockside; AFAR magazine].
- **Sense of place (verbatim from official site):** "The soaring city estate" / "Abound with creativity and alive with legacy."
- **Heritage context:** Built on the site of the former Holt's Wharf (dating to 1910), later New World Centre — the family property of Dr. Cheng Yu-tung and Dr. Henry Cheng, grandfather and father of Rosewood CEO Sonia Cheng [secondary: rosewoodhotels.com 2019 press release; Grokipedia].

### Useful real numbers
- **Rooms:** 413 total, including 91 suites [secondary: rosewoodhotels.com press release 2019; Wikipedia].
- **Branded residences:** 186 Rosewood Residences (studios through multi-floor apartments) co-located in the tower (official site).
- **Year opened:** March 17, 2019 [secondary: Rosewood press release].
- **Architect (tower):** Kohn Pedersen Fox (KPF) [secondary: Hospitality Design; Hotel Management].
- **Interior designer:** Tony Chi (tonychi, New York) — signature design language uses vaulted limestone walls, oak marquetry ceilings, coconut wood columns, marble floors, and lacquered panels with contemporary octagonal Chinese *bagua* motifs [secondary: rosewoodhotels.com press release; SCMP magazine profile].
- **Honors (from official property page):**
  - **No. 1 on The World's 50 Best Hotels 2025**
  - The Legacy House — MICHELIN Guide Hong Kong Macau **one-star** restaurant 2024–2026
  - CHAAT — MICHELIN Guide Hong Kong Macau **one-star** restaurant 2022–2026

## 3. Rosewood London

### Address + neighborhood + signature
- **Address:** 252 High Holborn, London WC1V 7EN, United Kingdom
- **Neighborhood:** Holborn — between the West End, the City, Covent Garden and Bloomsbury.
- **Sense of place (official):** "The Modern Manor House" — "blends a contemporary city escape with quintessential London grandeur."
- **Building heritage:** The former Pearl Assurance Company headquarters, a **Grade II listed** Edwardian Belle Époque building begun in 1912. Pearl Assurance HQ 1914–1989. Reopened as Rosewood London in **October 2013** after an £85M heritage restoration [secondary: Wikipedia — Rosewood London; A London Inheritance archive].

### Useful real numbers
- **Total rooms:** 262 guestrooms, including **44 suites** [secondary: Robb Report; Departures; visitlondon.com].
- **Year opened (as Rosewood):** October 2013.
- **Building architect (original):** H. Percy Monckton (central block 1912–1919); later additions by P. Monckton (east block, 1929–1930) and Bates & Sinning (mid-century extensions).
- **Hotel interior designer:** **Tony Chi and Associates** (signature suites) + **Martin Brudnizki** (public spaces and dining) [secondary: Hotel Management].
- **Honors:** Listed in Historic Hotels portfolios; "award-winning" classification used by Rosewood across multiple outlets. Specific Forbes/Condé Nast ranks `[not found on official site]`.

## 5. Pitch-Ready Facts Per Property

### Rosewood Hong Kong — 5 pitch-grade facts
1. **No. 1 on The World's 50 Best Hotels 2025** — current ranking, on the property landing page right now.
2. **413 rooms across 65 floors** of the KPF-designed Victoria Dockside tower, with interiors by **Tony Chi**, opened March 17, 2019.
3. **Two Michelin-starred restaurants on-property**: The Legacy House (Cantonese) and CHAAT (Indian street food), both with current 2024–2026 stars.
4. **Asaya Spa by Guerlain** — the flagship Asaya, 10 treatment rooms + 2,800 sq ft fitness center + infinity pool, with co-developed Guerlain signature treatments including "Spirit of Calligraphy."
5. **The Manor Club on the 40th floor** + two **Asaya Lodges** (each with a private treatment room) for guests wanting an integrated multi-day wellness stay — a model that exists at this scale nowhere else in the chain.

### Rosewood Sand Hill — 5 pitch-grade facts
1. **16 acres** at 2825 Sand Hill Road — 1 mile west of Stanford — opened 2009 by HKS Hill Glazier in California Ranch idiom, interiors by BAMO.
2. **121 rooms / 31 suites**, with a brand-new 2025 launch of six named **Residential Villas** (Ridge, Redwood, Verde, Orchard, Valley, Summit houses) designed by NICOLEHOLLIS with full chef's kitchens.
3. **Madera** — Michelin-starred Northern California restaurant under Chef Roman Petry, sourcing from Schmitz Ranch, Niman Ranch and Bay Area producers; the wood-fire room overlooks the Santa Cruz Mountains.
4. **"The Modern Clubhouse for Silicon Valley"** — the property markets itself as a working/social hub for the venture and tech community on Sand Hill Road, with a Lifestyle Membership program for Mid-Peninsula locals.
5. **Sense Spa with 13 treatment rooms** + outdoor heated pool, plus partnerships with **Ridge Vineyards** (Rosé Reveal experience) and **Flamingo Estate** (afternoon tea) that you can't replicate at other Rosewoods.

### Rosewood London — 5 pitch-grade facts
1. **A Grade II listed Edwardian Belle Époque building** at 252 High Holborn — the former Pearl Assurance HQ — reopened as Rosewood London in October 2013 after an **£85M restoration**.
2. **262 rooms, 44 suites**, with signature suites designed by **Tony Chi** and public spaces by **Martin Brudnizki**.
3. **The Manor House Suite (1,991 sq ft)** expands into **The Grand Manor House Wing**: a six-bedroom mega-suite with a **private entrance from High Holborn, private lift, and its own postcode** — believed to be the only hotel suite in the world with a dedicated postcode.
4. **Holborn Dining Room + The Pie Room + The Gin Bar** — a single venue that combines a Brudnizki-designed brasserie, a destination for craft British pies (signature: Beef Hotpot; Chicken & Wild Mushroom), and a gin program with **over 500 gins and 30+ tonics** (~14,000 advertised pairings).
5. **Mirror Room Art Afternoon Tea** (Monet-inspired) + **Scarfes Bar** built around Gerald Scarfe's original satirical illustrations — two named-IP cultural anchors you can't transplant.

## Notes for the demo data writer
- Suite naming is the cleanest cross-property memory hook: **Manor** appears at HK (Manor Suite / Manor Club) and London (Manor House Suite / Grand Manor House Wing). The demo can lean on this as a Rosewood-wide "manor" idiom.
- **Tony Chi** is the design through-line across HK (full property) and London (signature suites) — a credible "you liked Tony Chi's lacquered panels in Hong Kong, our London Manor House Suite is also his work" callback.
- **Asaya vs Sense** is the spa-tier distinction — Asaya is the elevated wellness sub-brand (HK flagship); Sense is the chain-wide standard (London + Sand Hill per the fact sheet). Sand Hill ambiguously uses both labels on its current site, so treat the fact sheet ("Sense®") as authoritative.
- Chefs change. Calum Franklin (HDR) left in 2022 — don't anchor a pitch to a specific chef name unless you've re-verified against the property's *current* dining sub-site within the last quarter.
