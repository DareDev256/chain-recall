# Rosewood Hotels & Resorts — Executive Briefing

**Purpose:** Reconnaissance for credible, respectful conversation if encountered at a Rosewood-adjacent event. Talking points are sourced from public statements, press releases, podcasts, and verified LinkedIn data. No private contact information is included.

**Product framing reference:** Chain Recall is an AI memory layer that sits on top of Oracle OPERA (and Hapi connectivity), reading existing guest profile + folio data and returning the "they had the cardamom latte last time, allergy to dairy, anniversary May 18" context to the on-duty staff member before the guest finishes their sentence. Zero rip-and-replace. ROI argument is preference-recall driving repeat ADR and NPS without adding service-cycle time.
